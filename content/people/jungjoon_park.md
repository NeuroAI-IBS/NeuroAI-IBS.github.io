Jungjoon Park is a Postdoctoral Researcher in the NeuroAI group at the IBS Center for Memory and Glioscience. Following his Ph.D. in Bio and Brain Engineering from KAIST (2022), he translated his systems and behavioral neuroscience expertise into practice by co-founding an AI startup for automated animal behavior analysis, and subsequently advanced next-generation multimodal behavior models as a postdoctoral researcher at KAIST AMILab. His research bridges behavioral neuroscience and multimodal AI to decode context-dependent information processing in both brain and behavior. He is particularly focused on how the principles of system consolidation—balancing episodic specificity with semantic generalization—and social cognition (e.g., empathy, Theory of Mind) can inspire cognitive architectures capable of cumulative learning and robust adaptive intelligence.
